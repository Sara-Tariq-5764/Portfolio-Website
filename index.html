Profile

I turn ambiguous problems into systems that run.

Most of the work I have owned has started without a clear system in place. I have worked across investment reporting, business development, operations, and analytics—often where data was fragmented, processes were manual, or decisions lacked a structured analytical foundation. My role has been to bring structure: understand the problem, establish reliable data, build the analysis, and turn it into something people can actually use.

That has become the pattern across five-plus years: get to the raw data, establish whether it can be trusted, build the analysis, and put it in front of the person making the decision. I don't see delivery discipline and analytical depth as separate jobs. The strongest solutions require both—the technical work has to be right, and the people, priorities, and execution around it have to work too.

Today, I work at the intersection of analytics, AI, and program delivery—coordinating enterprise AI initiatives while building analytical solutions that support operational and strategic decision-making.
